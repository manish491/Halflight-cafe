A cafe for the in-between hours

Half Light is built for the time between sunrise and the first lamp: a late breakfast, a long chat, an evening snack that turns into dinner. We pour specialty coffee next to frothy filter coffee, and fry our snacks to order.

  • Specialty coffee and filter coffee
  • Fresh juices and mint mocktails
  • Street snacks, sandwiches and desserts
  • Wi-Fi, power points and sunny window seats

Good to know

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome. Booking guarantees your table, especially on weekends and evenings.

Can I order for takeaway?

Yes. Choose Takeaway in the basket and send the order on WhatsApp.

Is there a discount code?

Use HALF10 in the basket for 10% off orders above ₹300.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Use the Veg only filter on the menu.
